## Official Website

[React - The library for web and native user interfaces](https://react.dev)

When you go to `react.dev/learn`, there are two titles in the sidebar: one is **Getting Started**, and the other is **Learn React**.

If you are a beginner, you should read the **Getting Started** section first, and then read the tutorials in **Learn React**.

# How do I learn (Especially when my English is not good)

## How to learn React and learn English by the way? (Applicable to all)

**The things you need**:

- A PC (That is the same as saying nothing 😂)
- Agent (Like Claude Code, Antigravity)
- A pen and a notebook, or an iPad with Apple Pencil

### Learn through video

Long time ago, I remember when I was in primary school grade 5:

> [!NOTE]
> When you are in grade 5, it means you have learned English for two years. We start to learn English when we are in grade 3.

That was a morning. Our English teacher usually had a listening test. Everyone prepared a pen and a piece of paper, listened carefully, and wrote down what they remembered on the paper as much as they could. Even though you could only catch 1 or 2 words, that was fine.

Until now, I still use that way to study English.

So let's see how we should use this in practice:

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Click that YouTube link on your PC or iPad, play the video, and write down some notes. After finishing, ask AI what these sentences or words mean.

Here is another question: you can ask AI, but AI cannot give you the best answer based on the video alone, or you have to write lots of prompts.

So my way to fix this is to download the video and use MacWhisper to convert the video to text. After getting the result, bring it to AI, and then keep asking questions to AI, and I'm sure it will give you a better answer—whatever the question is about, English or coding.

### Learn through Official Website

I think it's quite similar: make sure the agent is already installed on your PC, and open it.

Then ask AI to copy all the articles from the official website into your project.

After finishing, open your project and have a good conversation with AI. That's it.
